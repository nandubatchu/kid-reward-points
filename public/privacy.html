Privacy

Kid Reward Points is a browser-based family reward ledger. The Google Sheet you select or create remains in your Google account. This app has no developer-operated server that receives your Sheet contents.

Google access

When you connect Google, your browser receives a short-lived access token for files you create or select with the app. The token stays in browser memory and is not stored across page refreshes. You can disconnect the selected Sheet on this device in Settings. You can also revoke the app’s access in your Google Account permissions.

Information on this device

The browser stores the selected spreadsheet ID and name, display theme, a random client ID, any unfinished proposal, and a salted hash of a local device PIN. Clearing this site’s browser data removes these local settings. The PIN is a convenience check only; it does not prove parent identity or secure the Google Sheet from someone with direct access.

External services

The app loads Google Identity Services, Google Picker, Google Sheets API, and Google Fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the static app. These services may receive ordinary request information under their own policies. This app does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Contact

For questions about this app or your family’s data, contact the owner of the GitHub repository hosting this site. Your Sheet can be managed directly in Google Drive.
